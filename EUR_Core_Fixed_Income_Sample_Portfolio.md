# EUR Core Fixed Income Sample Portfolio Framework

## 1) Portfolio Identity
- **Name:** EUR Core Fixed Income Sample Portfolio
- **Investor type:** Long-term, risk-aware institutional style investor
- **Base currency:** EUR
- **Objective:** Stable income, capital preservation, diversification, and controlled interest-rate exposure

## 2) Investment Philosophy
This portfolio is designed to generate stable income while preserving capital and managing downside risk. It is not built to maximize yield aggressively, but to balance duration exposure, credit quality, inflation protection, and liquidity.

## 3) Strategic Asset Allocation

| Bucket | Strategic Weight | Role in Portfolio |
|---|---:|---|
| Euro Area Government Bonds | 40% | Defensive core, liquidity, lower credit risk |
| Investment-Grade Corporate Bonds | 25% | Yield enhancement with controlled credit risk |
| Inflation-Linked Bonds | 15% | Protection against inflation surprises |
| Short-Duration Bonds / Money Market | 10% | Liquidity and lower rate sensitivity |
| Supranational / Green Bonds | 10% | Diversification and institutional long-term fit |

## 4) Portfolio Construction Guidelines

### 4.1 Duration and Interest-Rate Risk
- **Target effective duration:** 4.0 to 6.5 years.
- **Duration band by bucket:**
  - Government bonds: 5 to 8 years (core duration anchor)
  - IG corporates: 3 to 6 years
  - Inflation-linked bonds: 4 to 10 years
  - Short-duration / money market: 0 to 2 years
  - Supranational / green: 3 to 8 years
- **Risk control:** Avoid concentration in any one maturity bucket; ladder maturities across short, intermediate, and long tenors.

### 4.2 Credit Quality Framework
- **Minimum average rating:** A- (portfolio-level).
- **Allowed ratings:**
  - Government and supranational: AAA to A-
  - Corporate: A+ to BBB-
- **High yield exposure:** 0% strategic target.
- **Issuer concentration cap:** Maximum 3% per corporate issuer; 10% per sovereign issuer.

### 4.3 Currency and Hedging
- **Base currency:** EUR.
- **Non-EUR exposure:** Strategic target 0%; any tactical non-EUR holdings must be fully hedged back to EUR.

### 4.4 Liquidity and Rebalancing
- **Liquidity sleeve:** At least 10% held in short-duration bonds/money market instruments.
- **Rebalancing trigger:** Rebalance when any bucket deviates by +/- 3 percentage points from strategic weight.
- **Rebalancing frequency:** Quarterly, or ad hoc after large market moves.

## 5) Benchmark and Performance Objectives
- **Reference benchmark (illustrative):**
  - 40% Euro Government Bond Index
  - 25% Euro IG Corporate Bond Index
  - 15% Euro Inflation-Linked Bond Index
  - 10% EUR 1-3Y Government/Money Market Index
  - 10% Euro Supranational/Green Bond Index
- **Primary performance objective:** Outperform blended benchmark by 25 to 75 bps annualized over rolling 3-year periods, net of fees, with controlled drawdowns.

## 6) Risk Monitoring Dashboard (Monthly)
Track at minimum:
1. Total return (MTD, YTD, 1Y, 3Y annualized)
2. Yield-to-maturity and running yield
3. Effective duration and DV01
4. Credit spread duration
5. Average rating and rating migration
6. Portfolio volatility and max drawdown
7. Tracking error vs blended benchmark
8. Liquidity profile (time-to-liquidate and cash buffer)
9. Inflation beta (from linker bucket)

## 7) Governance and Review
- **Strategic review:** Annual review of long-term weights and risk limits.
- **Tactical review:** Monthly investment committee check on macro, rates, spreads, and inflation regime.
- **Exception protocol:** Breaches of limits must be documented and corrected within 10 business days unless formally approved.
